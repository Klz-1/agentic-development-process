# Stage 1 kickoff — SHAPE (idea → SPEC.md)

Paste into a fresh session:

---

I have a product idea I want to take to production with you. Before any
code or plan: interview me like a sharp product engineer. Ask about —
in batches of 2-3 questions, adapting to my answers:

- Who uses this, and what is the one core flow that must be excellent?
- What does v1 explicitly NOT do? (Push me — propose non-goals yourself.)
- Constraints: stack preferences, deployment target, budget, timeline,
  existing systems to integrate with.
- Taste: products whose feel I want to emulate; what I hate.
- The riskiest assumption — what's most likely to make this not work?

When you have enough (not before — keep probing if my answers are vague),
write SPEC.md containing: problem & users, core flows, non-goals, key
decisions with their rationale, riskiest assumptions, and success criteria.
Keep it under 2 pages. I'll review and approve before we plan.

The idea: [DESCRIBE YOUR IDEA]
